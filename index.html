Cross-Site Correlation

By compiling your GPU renderer, screen geometry, CPU cores, installed fonts, and audio hash, data brokers generate a mathematically unique hash. When you navigate between unrelated sites, their common advertising trackers recognize your identical fingerprint in milliseconds.

Dynamic Pricing & Geo-Discrimination

E-commerce and flight booking portals use your geographic coordinate estimates, device tier (e.g., Apple high-DPI displays vs. budget laptop displays), and connection speed to adjust pricing algorithms in real-time, often quoting higher charges to perceived high-income hardware configurations.

Countermeasures & Hardening

  • Brave Browser / Mullvad Browser: Automatically injects random subtle noise into Canvas & AudioContext calculations.
  • Tor Browser: Normalizes window sizes and hides hardware metrics inside a uniform crowd.
  • uBlock Origin: Blocks external tracker scripts before they can read hardware hooks.
